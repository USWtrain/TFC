Required Training for Event Staff

This guide sets the standard way to run a US Wellness biometric and blood-draw event for Freeman Co., from arrival to Quest drop-off. It assumes you already know how to draw blood and take biometrics, and focuses on the Freeman-specific rules.

This training covers:

  • Event roles and who to go to for help
  • Participant flow and data capture in Salesforce
  • Lab requisitions and test codes
  • Tube labeling and barcode handling
  • Sample collection and centrifuging
  • Results forms
  • Packaging and Quest drop-off

Completion Requirements

  1. Read every module in this guide.
  2. Take the completion quiz. A score of 80% or higher passes.
  3. Send a screenshot of your certificate of completion to your manager.

Before the Event

  • Schedule, arrival time, and site details: check the USW scheduling app.
  • Supplies: all supplies will be shipped and brought onsite by event lead.
  • Quest hours: look up the nearest Quest location and when it closes. Samples go there the same day.
  • Dress code: all-black scrubs, comfortable closed-toe shoes, and socks. No Crocs.

Event Roles

RoleResponsibilities
Site LeadManages USW supplies, QCs samples and requisition forms, performs biometrics and draws, runs the end-of-day Salesforce review, and drops samples at Quest. First point of contact for all issues.
ScreenerPhlebotomist who completes blood draws and biometrics, and records them in Salesforce.
AdminCheck-in, paperwork, managing walk-ins, and assisting with biometrics.

Escalation Path

  1. Go to your Site Lead first for any question or problem.
  2. The Site Lead calls US Wellness at 866-881-7607 for:
    • Wi-Fi issues
    • Tablet failures
    • Supply shortages
    • Any issue that slows or stops participant flow
    • Any risk of missing the Quest drop-off

If no one answers, you must leave a voicemail with your name, the event name, and a description of the issue.

Critical Rules at a Glance

  • Upon arrival: connect to Wi-Fi and log in to Salesforce before the first participant arrives.
  • Requisitions: use Quest-branded requisition forms only. Mark tests with a check mark (✓), never an X.
  • Barcodes: barcode stickers come on each requisition. Apply one to each tube and one to the Other Notes section of the white USW results sheet. Enter the barcode number in Salesforce.
  • Labeling: label tubes at the chair with the participant present. Never pre-label.
  • Clotting: SST tubes clot upright for 30 minutes.
  • Centrifuge: SST tubes 10–15 minutes, within 60 minutes of collection.
  • Lavender tubes: do NOT centrifuge.
  • Red top tubes: discard only, with butterfly needles. Never send to the lab.
  • Results forms: white copy to US Wellness (barcode attached), yellow copy to the participant.
  • Storage: room temperature (ambient).
  • Quest drop-off: same day, before the nearest Quest location closes. Notify USW if you cannot make it.
  • Payment: participants pay nothing on site (payroll deduction).

Module 01: Event Overview & Participant Flow

Participants move through the stations in this order. Salesforce collects information in the same sequence.

  1. Height and Weight: BMI is calculated automatically.
  2. Waist Circumference: measure and record.
  3. Blood Pressure: measure and record.
  4. Venipuncture Blood Draw: collect the required specimens.

Module 02: Lab Requisition Form

Standard Testing Panel

Check these three tests on the Quest requisition form for every participant.

Requisition Form Handling

  • Complete every field highlighted in yellow for every participant.
  • Check the three test boxes above. Use a check mark (✓), not an X.
  • Keep one carbon copy for US Wellness.
  • Fold the remaining requisition paperwork and place it in the pouch of the biohazard bag.

Module 03: Tube Labeling

Required Identifiers

Each tube label must match the Quest requisition exactly:

  • Participant's first and last name
  • Date of birth
  • Quest barcode label

Label Tubes at the Chair

Before the participant leaves the chair:

  1. Complete the tube label, and attach the label and the barcode sticker.
  2. Read the information back to the participant.
  3. Verify accuracy.

If you run out of barcode stickers for tubes, use a blank label from your supply shipment and handwrite the barcode number.

Do Not

  • Pre-label tubes.
  • Leave full tubes unlabeled.
  • Carry unlabeled tubes away from the collection area.

Never submit a tube without the full first and last name and date of birth. The barcode links the sample to the record; the written identifiers verify the participant's identity.

Module 04: Sample Collection & Centrifuging

Collection Requirements

  • 2 Tiger Top SST tubes for every participant.
  • 1 Lavender Top tube for every participant.
Red top tubes are for discard use only, with butterfly needles. Do NOT submit them to the lab. Dispose of used red tops in the sharps container.

Processing Timeline

  1. Complete the draw and note the collection time on the Quest requisition form.
  2. Let SST tubes clot upright for 30 minutes.
  3. Centrifuge SST tubes for 10–15 minutes.
  4. Centrifuge within 60 minutes of collection.
Do NOT centrifuge Lavender Top tubes.

Recommended Workflow

  • Keep separate areas for tubes waiting to clot and processed tubes ready for storage.
  • Record collection times, or process tubes in the order they were drawn.

Module 05: Barcodes

Results Sheet

Apply a spare barcode sticker to the Other Notes section of the white US Wellness results sheet (shown in Module 07). If no stickers are left, neatly handwrite the barcode number.

Salesforce

Enter the barcode number in the Barcode Number field of the participant's Salesforce record (shown in Module 06).

Do Not

  • Submit a record without a barcode number.
  • Use one participant's barcode sticker on another participant's paperwork. A misplaced barcode creates errors for multiple participants.

Module 06: Completing the Salesforce Record

Complete the record before the participant leaves the station.

  1. Biometrics and Blood Pressure: verify all measurements are entered.
  2. Venipuncture Draw Completed: select Yes for every participant who had a draw.
  3. Number of Vials Drawn: record the number of tubes collected.
  4. Barcode Number: enter and verify.
  5. Participant Satisfaction Survey: the last three fields. The Screener asks the questions and records the answers.
  6. Submit Results: review everything, save, and select Submit Results.

Module 07: Results Forms

Every participant receives a completed results form.

CopyGoes toContains

What to Tell the Participant

"Your lab results will be available in the portal 1–2 weeks from today. This yellow copy has today's biometric measurements. It is not your lab report."

Do Not

  • Give the participant the white copy.
  • Return a white copy without a barcode.

Module 08: Packaging & Quest Drop-Off

Final Check Before Packaging Each Sample

  • Barcode attached
  • First and last name present
  • Date of birth present
  • Name and DOB match the requisition form exactly
  • SST tubes centrifuged
  • Lavender Top tube included (not spun)

Closing Procedures

  1. Verify each biohazard bag contains 2 spun SST tubes and 1 Lavender Top tube.
  2. Keep one carbon copy of each requisition and return it to USW with the white results sheets.
  3. Fold the remaining requisition paperwork into the pouch of the biohazard bag.
  4. Place specimens in the supplied laboratory transport bag and store at room temperature (ambient).
  5. The Site Lead drops samples at the nearest Quest Diagnostics location the same day, before it closes. Use the finder below to look up the closest location and its hours.
  6. If you can't make the drop-off, or Quest is closed or won't accept the samples, call US Wellness at 866-881-7607. If you reach voicemail, leave your name, the event name, and a description of the issue.

Find the Nearest Quest Location

Opens a map of nearby Quest Diagnostics locations. Check that the site accepts specimen drop-off and confirm its closing time before you go.

Overnight hold: if samples can't go to Quest that day, the Site Lead holds them overnight refrigerated. This is the only exception to room-temperature storage.

Module 09: End-of-Day Event Review ( Lead Responsibility)

Before leaving the site, the Site Lead confirms that Salesforce matches the samples collected.

  1. Count the biohazard bags and compare with the number of records marked Venipuncture Draw Completed = Yes.
  2. Confirm every record with a draw has a barcode number and has been submitted.
  3. Spot-check barcode numbers in Salesforce against the white results sheets.
  4. Fix any missing or mismatched record before leaving. Call USW if it can't be fixed on site.
